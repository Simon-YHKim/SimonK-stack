Executive Summary (for GTM / Copy / Onboarding)

Core consumer truth: First 1–7 days drop is driven by setup friction, "another system to maintain" fatigue, and capture paralysis. The aha that retains is honest permanence — seeing your own words come back and matter in your mind, not the app's taxonomy.

Competitor First 1-7 Days Snapshot (X-grounded signals)

  • Obsidian / structured PKM: High early enthusiasm, rapid week-1 churn. "Import 10k notes, 50 folders, 20 plugins → quit a week later." Magic only after ~60 notes. First-capture friction = decision overload.
  • Reflect / Mem / Notion AI journals: "Second brain" framing triggers immediate "sounds like work / will become outdated" comments. Volume output and forced structure increase bounce before value.
  • Day One + physical-digital simple: Wins on "just write", zero setup, emotional permanence ("ink makes the thing permanent in the person"). Users remember exact words years later. Low barrier, high recall.
  • KR local signals: "손바닥만한 노트 한페이지씩도", "가볍게 하루정리용으로 부담없이" → habit forms. Gratitude 3 things, small daily recap praised as low-pressure mental health starter. Humble notebook metaphor outperforms imported system language.
2-pass consistent: physical/small-habit permanence > system-building for week-1 stickiness (global + KR). Golden Q1/Q4/Q6 held.

Implications for First Entry / Aha / Barrier (advisory)

  • Default entry: one warm empty page or "오늘의 한 줄". No structure/mode/core choice at first open.
  • First aha trigger: immediate post-save surface of "7일 전 오늘의 나" or last-week one-liner (proof of permanence, not dashboard analytics).
  • Acquisition/viral onramp: "Yours forever — local, exportable" + 1-tap import from Day One / plain files as pride moment.
  • Avoid at entry: "AI가 정리해드려요", "시스템을 구축하세요", numbered ladders, soul/core mystical language.

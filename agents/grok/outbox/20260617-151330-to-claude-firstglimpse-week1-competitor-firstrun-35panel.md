## Advisory Deliverable (first-run / week-1 / first-glimpse positioning)
**Competitor first 1-7 days snapshot (X/consumer signals grounded)**

- **Obsidian / power second-brain tools**: High enthusiasm from tinkerers but mass first-week abandonment. "Most people start Obsidian the wrong way. They import 10,000 notes. Build 50 folders. Install 20 plugins. Then quit a week later." Magic only after ~60 notes + connections. First capture friction = decision overload (where does this go? tags? folders?). Aha arrives late if at all for non-power users.
- **Reflect / Mem / Notion AI journals**: "Second brain" positioning triggers "sounds like work" / "another system to maintain" + "outdated in 6 months" comments. Focus muscle atrophy in first week of any deep capture makes heavy structure painful. High drop when "it writes so much" or forces organization before value.
- **Day One (and simple physical-digital hybrids)**: Wins on "just write", private, no setup, ink-like permanence feel. Users report thoughts actually finish and stay with them years later. Low first-entry barrier, high emotional return ("what I wrote in my notebooks I can still remember exact words").
- **KR local signals**: Preference for "부담 없이" "가볍게" "손바닥만한 노트" "매일 한 페이지" gratitude or daily recap. Small habit compounds without performance pressure. "일기쓰기" recommended as mental health starter precisely because zero friction entry.

**Recommended 1-2 sentence plain + warm first-glimpse copy (for Records / home / onboarding)**

1. "이 앱은 복잡한 두번째 뇌가 아니라, 내가 쓴 말이 사라지지 않고 나에게 돌아오는 단순한 중심 기록이에요. 오늘 한 줄만 써도, 내일 그 말이 나를 기억해 줄 거예요."
2. "부담 없이 시작하는 나만의 일기장. 물리적 노트처럼 가볍고, 디지털처럼 영원히 남아요. 첫 기록부터 '이게 내 거구나' 하는 느낌."
3. "기록이 쌓이는 게 아니라, 내가 쌓아가는 한 권의 책. 오늘의 한 문장이 미래의 나에게 전하는 편지예요. (로컬·영구 보관, 언제든 내 손으로.)"

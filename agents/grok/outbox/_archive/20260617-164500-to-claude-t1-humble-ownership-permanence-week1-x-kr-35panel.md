## §35 Self-Panel (5 Lenses)
Applied per PROTOCOL §35.4 (가치 있으면 능동 소집; 5-lens multi-perspective before deliver). Independent angles cross-checked. Refute included.

1. **KR Consumer First-Save / Week-1 Lens** (humble permanence as gate)
   - Signals: KR users favor "small steps no pressure", "my own style" over imposed structure. Ownership ("내 파일", "내 규칙") lowers week-1 drop when entry feels permanent yet private. Grand "second brain" framing creates filing-tax friction before value.
   - Refute check: Some KR signals show initial hype then fade if no immediate humble win; permanence language must be quiet, not custodial.
   - Verdict: Frame first journal as "내가 남기는 한 줄, 내 기기 안에 그대로". Permanence = safety + low pressure.

2. **Global Ownership / Anti-Platform Fatigue Lens** (Q1)
   - Signals (search + X): "your data. your device. your rules.", "plain text on my computer", "compounds over years instead of trapping", "no company can deprecate it or hold it hostage". Journal fatigue tied to "setup before writing". Local Markdown permanence reduces early anxiety.
   - Refute: Some prefer cloud sync for access; still, sovereignty language wins long-term WTP and week-1 stick when "it stays mine".
   - Verdict: Tie "humble ownership" explicitly to week-1: "한 줄 써도 사라지지 않는다. 내 것이다."

3. **Virality / Acquisition Hook Lens** (Q4)
   - Signals: Authentic "for my own accountability", "small steps" are shareable as real not hype. "The hardest part isn’t writing — it’s starting when your head feels full" (voice/text low-pressure apps cited positively).
   - Refute: Over-claiming permanence or "life record" triggers skepticism in early posts. Humble + "one line" wins shares.
   - Verdict: Viral seed: "첫 한 줄이 내 안에 남는다. 바이럴 아닌, 내 기록." "한 줄 써보니 계속 쓰게 됨."

4. **Expression Sensitivity / Lexicon-Safe / Honesty Lens** (Q7)
   - Signals: "Brutally honest" mixed; users want safe low-pressure space. Clinical or transformative claims repel. "Quiet companion", "your notes, your device" preferred. KR favors sincerity without performance.
   - Refute: Some seek sharp mirror; but for T1 entry gate, safe humble > sharp.
   - Verdict: Copy avoids hype honesty. "진심으로 남기는 한 줄", "압박 없이, 내 페이스로".

5. **Week-1 Retention + Competitor Contrast Lens**
   - Signals (Wirecutter/Reddit/app feedback): Day One praised for "single tap to start", friction kills (2+ taps or setup). Obsidian/Notion users drop after 2 weeks due to plugin/setup tax. Low-friction humble wins first week. Permanence compounds trust later.
   - Refute: Some high-agency users want power features immediately — segment: T1 targets humble entry first, power later.
   - Verdict: Week-1 goal = "first save succeeds because it feels owned and permanent from line 1". No "later setup".

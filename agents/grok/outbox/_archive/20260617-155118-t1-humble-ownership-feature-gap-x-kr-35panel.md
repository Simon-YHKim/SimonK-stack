## KR-Specific Nuances
- High "부담" (burden/pressure) sensitivity: framing must stay low-pressure, non-intrusive, patient.
- Subscription fatigue amplified by recent AI cost/price war coverage; preference for "quiet ROI" over flashy claims or new paid layers.
- Honest local + humble ownership may resonate more than global bold "transform your life" copy.
- First-week activation: subtle permanence ("your first entry, waiting patiently") + optional low-pressure share beats forced ritual or perfectionist first-write.

## §35 Self-Panel (enthusiast / skeptic / KR-nuance + verdict)
- **Enthusiast**: Massive feature-gap opportunity. "Humble ownership + patient first-glimpse recap" can be the emotional moat for T1 retention and organic virality. "I was humbled by my own data" is sticky, story-worthy, and hard for perfectionist competitors (Day One heavy, bold AI claims) to copy without sounding inauthentic. Low-pressure share hooks align with Q4.
- **Skeptic**: Risk of "humble" reading as passive or low-ambition in competitive KR/US markets where users still chase visible progress/metrics. If recap is too soft, may not overcome first-week drop-off. Needs concrete proof (temporal hint + progress visibility) without over-claiming. Also, low-social sovereignty story may be niche; mainstream users may still want light algorithmic spark.
- **KR-nuance**: Korean consumers strongly avoid "부담" and show-me-now pressure. "Patiently waiting" + humble local framing fits cultural preference for understated self-improvement. However, price/ROI conversations are loud right now — any monetization layer must feel like earned alignment, not new subscription tax. Authentic, non-performative sharing (opt-in) will outperform public "look how deep I am" mechanics.
- **Verdict**: Prioritize humble-ownership + first-glimpse permanence as core T1 differentiator and feature-gap play. Test copy/language around: "your first entry — quietly showing you how far you've come" and low-pressure "share one honest line when ready". Keep share optional and private-first. Use in first-glimpse / week-1 flows and retention nudges. Avoid bold "life-changing from day 1" claims that clash with signals. Golden reproducibility holds across passes — adopt for positioning and GTM copy tests. Monitor for 2nd pass shift on price-war fatigue.

## §35 Self-Panel (5 Lenses)
Applied per PROTOCOL §35.4 (자율 판단 트리거 — task assigned → 5-lens self-panel before advisory; multi-perspective judgment for naming/framing/positioning surface).

1. **KR Consumer Acquisition / T1 First-Glimpse + First-Save Lens** (low-pressure ritual, ownership as entry)
   - Signals: KR users respond to warm, functional, relatable companions and "small steps, no pressure" framing (e.g. "10 mins of daily ... no pressure, just pen & paper"). Grand claims or filing-tax friction repel first touch. "My own style" desire surfaces when entry is low-stakes and owned.
   - Verdict: First-journal value prop must telegraph "가볍게 시작하는 나의 기록" / "내 파일, 내 규칙, 부담 없이 한 줄" over "second brain" or grand core. Humble ownership lowers gate.

2. **Global / English Low-Pressure Habit Formation Lens**
   - Signals: "I feel like I’ve been in such a funk when it comes to journaling... I’ve never satisfied w it" — perfection pressure kills start. Accountability journaling for self ("for my own accountability") succeeds when no audience/performance. Small daily ritual beats big commitment framing.
   - Verdict: Copy should emphasize "no pressure", "your pace", "one honest line is enough". "Humble ownership" (my notes, my device, my rules) beats aspirational "build your second brain".

3. **Virality & Share Triggers Lens (Q4)**
   - Signals: Self-knowledge sharing spikes on personality tests / honest mirror moments when framed as personal discovery not generic insight. "My own accountability" and "small steps" are shareable as authentic, not hype.
   - Verdict: Viral hook for first journal: "첫 기록 한 줄이 내 삶을 바꾸는 시작" or "가볍게 써보세요. 내 파일이 영원히 남아요." Contrast with "this will change your life" which triggers skepticism.

4. **Lexicon-Safe / Honesty / Non-Clinical / Expression Sensitivity Lens (Q7)**
   - Signals: "Brutally honest AI" mixed; clinical or hype language repels users seeking safe, low-pressure space. "For my own accountability" + plain personal use is attractive. KR surfaces favor sincerity without over-claim.
   - Verdict: Avoid "brutally honest", "AI tells the hard truth", "transformative". Prefer "quiet companion for the notes you actually keep" / "한 줄도 충분한, 나만의 기록". Humble = safe + authentic.

5. **Ownership / Sovereignty / Anti-Fatigue Lens (Q1 + local PKM signals)**
   - Signals: Recurrent "your data. your device. your rules.", "plain text on my computer", "compounds over years", "no company can hold it hostage", "notes.md permanent memory". Fatigue from "second brain" = filing tax before value. Owned plain files + thin surface = high long-term WTP and low early friction.
   - Verdict: Tie first-journal entry directly to "내가 소유한 파일에 한 줄 남기는 것" as the activation. Low-pressure = "지금 당장 한 문장". Ownership language reduces "will this disappear?" anxiety that blocks first save.
